Each process has at most64 entries/32MiB, each entry at most2MiB and60 seconds from its original observation; access never renews expiry and restart always misses. Entries contain data, never authority decisions, credentials or binary buffers.

Every hit requires fresh online host authority at entry and final disclosure, exact current custody/binding/client/generation, valid runtime-owned source nomination and current row/durable mailbox generations.
